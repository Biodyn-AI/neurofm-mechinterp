\noindent\textbf{One Anchor per Source: Hyperspherical Geometry and Causal Roles of the
Latent Sources in an EEG/MEG Foundation-Model Tokenizer}

\medskip
\noindent Ihor Kendiukhov

\bigskip
\begin{itemize}
\item Each latent source of an EEG/MEG tokenizer occupies a cap around a learned anchor
\item The anchor is the leading uncentered, not centered, principal direction of a source
\item Decoder keys read where a source lies relative to anchors; values read its content
\item With a decoder source embedding (two training runs), the anchors do not separate
\item Tokens retain diagnostic information, not concentrated in anchors within cohorts
\end{itemize}
